# Makecord brand

- `logo.svg`: the full logo. A white M on the blue disk (`#0024D5` → `#00136F` radial).
- `logo-mark.svg`: the M alone, white on transparent, for placing on your own background.

The M is a single 8.5-unit stroke with round caps and joins, symmetric about x = 50. The two
dots at the feet are patch-cable jacks (r = 7). Don't redraw it as an outline. Edit the stroke path.
