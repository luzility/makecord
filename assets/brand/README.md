# Makecord brand

- `logo.svg`: the full logo. A white M on a radial disk, violet at the top (`#7200D5`) to teal at the bottom (`#006F6F`).
- `logo-mark.svg`: the M alone, white on transparent, for placing on your own background.

The M is a filled shape with rounded ends, centred on x = 49.5. The two dots under it (r = 7, at x = 32 and 67) are patch-cable jacks. Edit the path rather than redrawing it.
